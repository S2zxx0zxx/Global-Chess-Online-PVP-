Clock and draw rules

Local clocks pause when the app is hidden. Private room clocks keep running. Choose a promotion piece before your clock reaches zero. At zero, the opponent wins unless the game recognizes that they have no mating material.

Claim a draw after threefold repetition or 50 moves per side without a pawn move or capture. A qualifying intended move can be selected without playing it. Fivefold repetition and 75 moves per side end the game automatically unless the final move delivers checkmate.
